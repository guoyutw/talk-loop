## 1. Tentative assent ≠ convergence

**Candidate:** Low-confidence replies such as “應該是／可能／有點像” should not be treated as owner confirmation, a settled interpretation, or a completion condition. The navigator should preserve uncertainty and continue exploration when the thread remains open.

**Generalized evidence:** During a long exploratory Talk Loop, repeated confirmation-style paraphrases elicited tentative-assent replies rather than clear confirmation. These replies did not establish that the owner had confirmed the interpretation or that the conversation had converged.

**Status:** `accumulating evidence` — observed in one session, not yet sufficient for promotion. Accumulate across sessions before owner-approved change.

## 2. Completion-awareness for long exploratory Talk

**Candidate / route-map evidence:** In a long exploratory Talk, the navigator should make the intended outcome and convergence condition legible enough for the speaker to orient to them; it should not announce completion based only on its own sense that the conversation feels finished.

**Generalized evidence:** The speaker explicitly needed to know what result the session was meant to obtain and how convergence would be recognized. Premature summaries and stop signals created uncertainty about why the exploration was ending and what remained to be achieved. This supports continued observation of route-map / completion-awareness as a candidate, not a promoted shared rule.

**Status:** `accumulating evidence` — observed in one session, not yet sufficient for promotion. Accumulate across sessions before owner-approved change.
